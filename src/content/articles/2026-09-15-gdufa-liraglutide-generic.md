## Key Takeaways

- FDA used GDUFA-supported analytical and in silico methods to underpin approval of the first generic liraglutide.
- These are the first once-daily GLP-1 peptide generics approved in the U.S.
- FDA published revised draft product-specific guidances for liraglutide to further guide generic development.

## What drove approval of generic liraglutide?

Generic liraglutide entered FDA's generic drug program as a complex peptide. GDUFA funding enabled FDA to develop analytical and in silico methods that characterized peptide structure, impurity profiles, and higher-order attributes for Victoza and Saxenda analogs. Those methods supported FDA's approval of the first once-daily GLP-1 generic peptides in the U.S. ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/media/194020/download?attachment=&utm_source=openai))

## When did FDA approve these generic GLP-1 peptides?

FDA approved the first generic referencing Victoza (liraglutide injection) on December 23, 2024, granted to Hikma Pharmaceuticals USA Inc. This marked the first once-daily GLP-1 generic peptide for type 2 diabetes ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-generic-once-daily-glp-1-injection-lower-blood-sugar-patients-type-2-diabetes?utm_source=openai)). FDA's research program had already advanced peptide analytical methods in FY 2023 ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/media/177974/download?utm_source=openai)).

## How is FDA guiding future generic peptide development?

On July 28, 2026, FDA published revised draft product-specific guidances for peptide generics including liraglutide (Victoza, Saxenda), covering impurity thresholds, higher-order structure, and innate immune testing recommendations ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-publishes-revised-draft-product-specific-guidances-certain-generic-peptide-products?utm_source=openai)). FDA continues advisory workshops on GDUFA science priorities and issued performance goals under GDUFA III to enable complex generic approval pathways ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/generic-drugs/generic-drug-research-priorities-projects?utm_source=openai)).

## FAQ

### What is generic liraglutide?

Generic liraglutide is a once-daily GLP-1 peptide injection that matches the reference listed drugs Victoza (for type 2 diabetes) or Saxenda (for weight management). It received FDA approval through the ANDA pathway, supported by GDUFA research.

### How did GDUFA research help approve generic liraglutide?

GDUFA-funded analytical and in silico studies characterized peptide structure, folding, impurity profiles, and higher-order forms of liraglutide. This enabled FDA to assess bioequivalence without full clinical trials.

### When did generic liraglutide receive FDA approval?

FDA approved the first generic injection referencing Victoza on December 23, 2024 ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-generic-once-daily-glp-1-injection-lower-blood-sugar-patients-type-2-diabetes?utm_source=openai)).

### What guidance does FDA offer for generic peptide developers?

FDA published revised draft product-specific guidances on July 28, 2026, covering liraglutide, Saxenda, and other peptides, with recommendations on structure, impurities, and immunogenicity testing ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-publishes-revised-draft-product-specific-guidances-certain-generic-peptide-products?utm_source=openai)).

### Are more once-daily GLP-1 generics expected soon?

FDA stated it expects more generic approvals in this class as interest grows and GDUFA-supported methods enable complex peptide ANDA submissions ([fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov/media/194020/download?attachment=&utm_source=openai)).
